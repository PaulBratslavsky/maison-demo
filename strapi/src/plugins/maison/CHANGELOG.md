# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- **Customer inquiries: every concierge turn is recorded, a model labels it in the background, and staff work the queues and reply on LINE.** A proof of concept, built on the customer questions below.
  - `log_inquiry` (MCP: log customer inquiries) records one concierge turn for the signed-in customer: their message and the concierge's reply, whether knowledge was found, whether the turn handed the question off, and optionally the question the hand-off recorded, the piece and the chat's language. The app's server calls it after each turn. The text keeps its line breaks, the message cut to 1,000 characters and the reply to 2,000. An existing customer token needs the new permission, and the admin chat doesn't offer the tool.
  - A new content type, `plugin::maison.inquiry`, hidden from the Content Manager and the Content-Type Builder because it holds customers' LINE IDs, and an `inquiries` service.
  - **Labelling.** A cron job, `maison-label-inquiries`, runs every minute and labels up to 10 inquiries, oldest first, through the AI SDK: a kind (question, complaint, praise or other), a sentiment, whether the concierge answered, and the model's reason and topic, in English. The prompt treats the customer's text as evidence, not instructions. A model that answers in the wrong shape fails that inquiry with nothing half-written saved, at most 5 times, and **Label again** puts it back.
  - **New config:** `aiProvider` (`anthropic`, `openai` or `openai-compatible`), `aiModel`, `aiApiKey` and `aiBaseUrl`, read from `AI_PROVIDER`, `AI_MODEL`, `AI_API_KEY` and `AI_BASE_URL`. The default is Claude Haiku 4.5. **Without a key, nothing is sent to a model:** new inquiries are marked `skipped` and wait under Not labelled, and the sweep labels them once a key is set. The plugin now needs Node 22.12 or later, which the AI SDK needs.
  - **Queues, by code.** A hand-off, or a question the concierge didn't answer, is in Needs an answer, a complaint is in Complaints, and praise is in Praise. A person's label wins over the model's.
  - **An Inquiries tab on the Maison page,** for admins with the new permission "Review customer inquiries". It refreshes every 5 seconds, and shows four cards that count the open inquiries (Needs an answer, Complaints, Praise and Not labelled), the same five filters as pills, and each inquiry's time, masked customer, message, piece, kind, sentiment and status, with **What the concierge said** for its reply and the model's reason and topic. The messages keep their line breaks, and a long one scrolls in its box. A hand-off shows its question's reference and points to Questions. Above the table, the month's LINE messages, which replies count toward.
  - Admins with the new permission "Reply to customer inquiries on LINE" get **Reply on LINE**, **Close** (Answered elsewhere, Not needed or Spam), **Change label** and **Label again**.
    - **Reply on LINE** opens a dialog with a box that is never pre-filled, and **Use the suggested text** for a complaint or for praise, in English or Japanese. It pushes one LINE text message in Maison's name, quoting the customer's first 80 characters, and marks the inquiry replied. The admin's first name is saved on the inquiry and never sent. It is disabled while a request runs.
    - A hand-off is answered under Questions, not here.
    - **Change label** sets the kind, the sentiment or both, and the inquiry is then a person's: the sweep never labels it again. **Label again** isn't offered for it.
  - **The Maison page has tabs now:** Requests, Questions and Inquiries, each shown to the admins who may see what is in it, with Demo data below them.
  - **A second Homepage widget,** "Maison inquiries" (`plugin::maison.inquiries`), for admins with "Review customer inquiries": the four open counts, refreshed every 5 seconds. The requests widget is unchanged.
  - **Admin routes:** `GET /maison/inquiries` (with `filter` and `limit`), `GET /maison/inquiries/summary` and `GET /maison/inquiries/quota`, and `POST /maison/inquiries/:documentId/reply`, `/close`, `/label` and `/label-again`. A 200 carries the server's `message`, which warns when the customer has the reply but recording it failed. Errors are 400 (`invalid_input`), 404, 409 (`already_closed`, `already_replied`, `use_question` and `not_failed`), 502 when LINE refused the message or couldn't be reached, and 503 without `lineChannelAccessToken`. The new error codes are `already_closed`, `already_replied` and `not_failed`.
  - **Reset demo appointments and questions** also deletes every inquiry. `POST /maison/demo/reset` answers `{ appointments, notifications, questions, inquiries, knowledge }`.
- **Customer questions: when the concierge can't answer, staff follow up on LINE.** Strapi records the question, staff let the customer know or answer on LINE in their own name, and an answer can become product knowledge. A proof of concept.
  - `hand_off_to_staff` (MCP: hand questions to staff) records the signed-in customer's question for Maison's client advisors, with the piece it's about and the customer's LINE name when LINE gives one, and answers a reference like `Q-4821`. It sends the customer nothing. A customer can have five questions open or taken at a time: another is refused with the new error code `too_many_open_questions` (MCP only: no REST route hands off a question). An unknown piece never refuses a hand-off. An existing customer token needs the new permission, and the admin chat doesn't offer the tool.
  - A new content type, `plugin::maison.question`, hidden from the Content Manager and the Content-Type Builder because it holds customers' LINE IDs, and a `questions` service.
  - A **Customer questions** section on the Maison page, for admins with the new permission "Read customer questions": it refreshes every 5 seconds and filters to Open, Answered or All. Admins with "Answer customer questions on LINE" get **Let them know**, which sends the customer one LINE message in their first name and takes the question, and **Answer**, a dialog whose **Send on LINE** sends the answer in their name and marks the question answered. With **Add to product knowledge** ticked, the answer is also published as a product knowledge entry in the question's language, with the question's piece as its product and a title staff can edit, the customer's question to start with.
  - `GET /maison/questions`, `POST /maison/questions/:reference/notify` and `POST /maison/questions/:reference/answer`. A 200 carries the server's `message`, which can warn that recording the question failed or that the entry couldn't be made, as the customer has the message by then. Errors are 400, 404, 409 (`already_taken`, `already_answered`), 502 when LINE refused the message or couldn't be reached, which leaves the question as it was, and 503 without `lineChannelAccessToken`.
  - The messages are in the question's language, Japanese or English, quote the question cut to 80 characters, and are signed with the admin's first name, or write for the team without one. The code that pushes to LINE is now shared by confirmations and staff messages. Confirmations are unchanged.
  - **Reset demo appointments and questions** also deletes every question and the product knowledge entries their answers added, in every language. The seeded product knowledge stays. `POST /maison/demo/reset` answers `{ appointments, notifications, questions, inquiries, knowledge }`.
  - After review: the Answer dialog shows the knowledge entry's public title as **Title in product knowledge**, for staff to edit (`title` in the answer's body), an admin whose first name is the house's own ("Maison", or either `houseName`) writes for the team, `hand_off_to_staff` answers the existing reference for a question the customer still has with staff instead of recording it twice, and a 200 with `warning: true` shows as a warning.
- **Product knowledge, and `search_knowledge`.** A new content type, `plugin::maison.knowledge`, localized with draft and publish: a title, an answer of up to 2,000 characters, a category, the products it's about (`productSlugs`, empty for every piece) and keywords. Saving one refuses a `productSlugs` that isn't a list of distinct slugs.
  - `search_knowledge` (MCP: browse the catalog) returns the best four published entries for a question, in its locale with the default locale filling in, scored on the question's words in the title, the keywords and the answer, or none. With `productSlugs`, entries about other pieces are left out, and an unknown or unpublished product is `not_found`.
  - `GET /api/maison/knowledge` is the same search over REST, on the action `plugin::maison.knowledge.find`.
  - A question is trimmed before it's checked, so one of only spaces is refused like an empty one: `invalid_input` over REST, and the MCP SDK's own `Input validation error` over MCP.
  - **Load demo catalog** adds 16 product knowledge entries in English, also to a catalog loaded before. Its notice says what it added.
- **Strapi sends the LINE confirmation itself, on every confirm path:** the board's Confirm, `confirm_appointment` in the admin chat or from an MCP client, and Publish in the Content Manager.
  - Once a publish of an appointment has gone through, a document middleware pushes the visit's flex message with LINE's push API and waits for the answer, 8 seconds at most. Inside a transaction, as the Content Manager's Publish runs, it sends once the transaction commits instead, without waiting, and a rolled-back publish sends nothing. A failed send never fails the publish. The new `line-confirmations` service sends it, with the message `pending_confirmations` lists, now built by one shared function.
  - Each attempt is recorded as a notification by `strapi`: sent, or failed with LINE's HTTP status and message. A push LINE took but that couldn't be recorded answers `sent_unrecorded`. A visit gets one, so publishing again sends nothing, two sends at once share one push, and a visit that's over gets none. `confirmations.record()` takes an optional `recordedBy`, and still writes `ops-agent` without it.
  - A new appointment's reference is never one a notification still names, so a new visit can't count as already confirmed over LINE.
  - New config: `lineChannelAccessToken`, from `LINE_CHANNEL_ACCESS_TOKEN`, and `lineApiBaseUrl`, `https://api.line.me` by default. Without a token, Strapi sends and records nothing, and logs a warning once.
  - **Send again** on the board, for confirmed requests not yet sent whose visit is still ahead, through `POST /maison/appointments/:reference/notify`, gated like Confirm.
  - The confirm toast says whether the customer's LINE confirmation was sent, and points to Send again when it wasn't. It and `confirm_appointment`'s description no longer say the ops agent sends the confirmation. The ops agent's tools and prompt are unchanged, for retries.
- **The LINE confirmation is in the language the customer booked in.** An appointment keeps it as `language` (`ja` or `en`, `ja` by default), from the booking's `locale`, or `defaultLocale` without one. Every confirmation follows it, from Strapi or `pending_confirmations`: the words, the house name, the date (`Sat 10 Oct, 14:00` in English, from the new `formatEnDateTime`), and the boutique's name and address and the pieces' names, from their published versions in that language with the default locale's value for a field that's missing or empty. A visit without a `language`, such as one booked before, is Japanese, and a Japanese confirmation is exactly what it was.
- **A "Maison requests" widget on the admin Homepage** (`plugin::maison.requests`), for admins with "MCP: review appointment requests". It counts one pipeline of visits still ahead (the requests waiting for staff, the confirmed visits, and how many of those have had their LINE confirmation sent), lists the five newest requests, and refreshes every 5 seconds.
  - `GET /maison/appointments/summary`, an admin route gated on the same permission as the board's list. It answers `{ counts, recent }` from the new `appointments.summarizeRequests()`, which counts with the board's own definitions and returns the board's "All requests" rows, masked the same way.
- **REST routes at `/api/maison`**, on the same services, input schemas and LINE customer identity as the MCP tools:
  - `GET /collections`, `GET /products`, `GET /products/:slug` and `GET /boutiques`, as `browse_collections`, `search_products`, `view_product` and `find_boutiques`. Their actions are `plugin::maison.collections.find`, `products.find`, `products.findOne` and `boutiques.find`. Grant them to a role such as Public, or call with a read-only, full-access or custom API token.
  - `POST /appointments` and `GET /my-appointments`, as `request_appointment` and `my_appointments`, for the signed-in LINE customer only. A new `customer-session` policy runs `/mcp`'s session check (oauth-mcp-manager's `resolveAccessToken`), then resolves the customer the way the tools do (`resolveSubject`). It answers 401 with `WWW-Authenticate: Bearer` when that check refuses the session or no LINE customer holds it, 503 `not_configured` without oauth-mcp-manager 1.1, and 503 `temporarily_unavailable` when checking the session fails on the server.
  - `/mcp` goes further in two ways. Strapi core also refuses a session whose admin token has expired (`checkExpiry`), which the REST door accepts until the session itself expires, at most the session TTL of 1 hour. And each tool needs its own permission on that admin token, which the REST door doesn't check. Closing the first gap belongs in oauth-mcp-manager (`resolveAccessToken` refusing an expired admin token), in a later release.
  - Errors as `{ "error": { "code", "message", "hint" } }`, with the tools' codes, messages and hints, and statuses 400, 401, 404, 409, 422 and 503. One code is the REST door's own: `temporarily_unavailable`.
- `createdVia: "web"` for visits requested through the REST routes. The requests board, `appointment_requests` and `confirm_appointment` show it.
- `identity.customerSession(authorization)`: the customer behind an `Authorization` header, for the MCP tools and the REST routes alike. It always runs `/mcp`'s session check first. Only the MCP tools skip that step, inside the service, because `/mcp` has already run it on their request.

### Changed

- Answering a customer question marks the inquiries its hand-off came from as replied, with the answer, when, and by whom. A failure to mark them never changes the outcome of the answer: the customer has it, and the server logs a warning.
- `request_appointment` takes an optional `locale` (`ja` or `en`), like the other customer tools, and names the boutique and products in its answer in that language. Until now it always answered in `defaultLocale`, which is still the default. The booking body of `POST /api/maison/appointments` takes it too.
- The customer tools' input schemas live in `server/src/mcp/schemas.ts`, shared with the REST routes. Apart from `request_appointment`'s `locale`, the tools' inputs, outputs and errors are unchanged.
- A failed customer lookup is logged without oauth-mcp-manager's error message, which could quote the token.
- Demo catalog photos from Pexels replace all eighteen generated images. See `server/seed/images/SOURCES.md`.
- The LINE confirmation message uses the app's black-and-white palette: an ink header with the house name in white, grey labels and an ink button, in place of the cream on near-black.
- The Homepage widget shows its three counts as cards and its five newest requests as a table, with when each request came in and the customer's note, and the Maison page shows the same cards above the board, whose rows show the note too. The `recent` rows of `GET /maison/appointments/summary` gain `createdAt` and `note`.
